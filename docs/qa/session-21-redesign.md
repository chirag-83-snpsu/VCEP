## 1. Executive Summary & Design Rationale

Undergraduate commerce resumes frequently suffer from fatal credibility defects: candidates make unverified, inflated claims (such as *"Finance expert who improved company profit 40% and managed corporate accounts"*), which immediately collapse during technical interviews or corporate background verification. In the approved canonical scenario at Vayentra Labs:
- **The Core Problem:** The student's actual work was an intensive, supervised academic simulation without measured live enterprise profit impact.
- **The Authentic Solution:** Credible resumes articulate verifiable commercial capabilities using the **Google XYZ Formula** (*Accomplished [X] as measured by [Y] by doing [Z]*), while explicitly disclosing the `[Simulated Project]` context.
- **Verifiable Portfolio Index:** Every resume claim links directly to an authorized student deliverable across six core curriculum domains (Accounting, Reconciliation, MIS, Invoice review, AI verification, and Capstone), complete with file names (`StudentID_S*.xlsx/docx`) and cryptographic SHA verification hashes.
- **45-Second Recruiter Defense:** In a high-pressure 45-second recruiter audit simulation, the candidate immediately surfaces the exact mathematical calculation proof and auditable workbook references, followed by a transparent appraisal of one target role skill gap and next practice task.
