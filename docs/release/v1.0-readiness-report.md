## 3. Operational Constraints for College Pilot

1. **Synthetic Staging Environment**:
   - The pilot must be deployed to a private staging worker (e.g. `https://staging-worklab.vcep.edu` or Cloudflare Workers staging route).
   - Database must be initialized with synthetic college cohorts (College Alpha, College Beta) using `scripts/seed-staging.mjs`. No production student data may be migrated to staging.
2. **Spreadsheet Upload Policy**:
   - Because the upload validator currently accepts `PNG`, `JPEG`, `PDF`, and `DOCX`, students building Excel financial models (Sessions 05, 07, 08, 10, 11, 12) must export their sheets to PDF or embed summary tables in DOCX before uploading evidence.
3. **Dual-Curriculum Training Briefing**:
   - Trainers must brief students that **Guided Learning** (Drive Edition 3.0) is the classroom lab simulation based on Vayentra Labs, while the **Assessed Worksheet** tests transfer of learning to Indian enterprise cases (HUL, Infosys, Maruti, Titan).
